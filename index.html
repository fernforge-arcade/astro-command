ASTRO COMMAND

A real-time strategy game

How to play

GoalDestroy the enemy's Command Center. Don't lose yours.
SelectLeft-click a unit, or drag a box. Shift-click to add.
Move / ActRight-click to move, attack, gather, or build.
CameraArrow keys / screen-edge / minimap to pan. Wheel to zoom.
Attack-MovePress A then click a destination.
Stop / HoldS stop · H hold position.
Control groupsCtrl+1..9 assign · 1..9 recall.
BuildSelect a Worker → pick a building → place it on the map.
EconomySelect a Worker, then right-click a ◆ mineral patch to mine. New to RTS? Try the Tutorial on the menu.
PauseP or Esc.
